About Jatin Electronics

27 Years of Manufacturing Excellence & Innovation

Our Story

Founded in 1997 by Mr. Akshay Kumar Mali, Jatin Electronics began with a clear vision: to make Udaipur a prominent hub for electronics manufacturing. What started as a dedicated small operation has grown into a trusted partner for major industrial clients across India.

For over 27 years, we have maintained our unwavering commitment to zero-defect product manufacturing while continuously expanding our capabilities, expertise, and client base.

Today, Jatin Electronics stands as an industry leader in contract manufacturing, known for exceptional reliability, uncompromising quality, and professional service.
